The archive

Every card is drawn live in your browser by the same engine that generated the collection — from nothing but its token id, species and seed. Open one to watch it move.

How the engine works

A custom renderer, not image generation. Each species is a procedural rig of signed-distance shapes with its own anatomy and motion; the rasterizer turns depth, light and contour direction into characters.

Shape → glyph

Contours become / | \ - by their direction, lit volumes become dense glyphs like $ 8 B @, shadows thin out to . : '. White, grey and a restrained orange — nothing else.

Seamless motion

Breathing, wingbeats, swimming waves, tail swings and glyph drift are all functions of one loop phase with whole-number frequencies, so every loop closes perfectly. Verified for all 777 tokens.

Verified uniqueness

Tokens of the same species must differ in at least two structural traits — never just colour — and pass a perceptual-hash check plus visual QC: subject in frame, eye visible, coverage in range.
